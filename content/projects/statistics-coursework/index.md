### Running Training Study: The Islands — DASC 201

For my intro to data science project, I designed and ran an experiment on [The Islands](https://islands.smp.uq.edu.au/) — a virtual population platform used for teaching statistics and research methods. I recruited 40 virtual subjects, assigned them to different running training plans, and measured whether the training affected their aerobic fitness. The results were surprising and not what I expected based on my own experience as a collegiate runner.
